## Accepted OEM repair scope

Exact Yamaha D03-2819U-10_02 source SHA256 b5cbf756d688c8516251e456d4255da4ab607c8e1206e1408f254a79f74a6fbd. PDF168 identifies damper and base-valve assemblies separately from the outer tube, inner tube, spring, scraper, bushings and seals. PDF170 directs replacement of bent/damaged damper assemblies. PDF171 directs base-valve replacement for damage or spring fatigue, cleaning for contamination, and inspection/replacement of its O-rings, bushing and bleed screw. PDF171–177 supports chamber filling, bleeding, travel/leak acceptance and stock external reassembly.

Assembly replacement is legitimate OEM repair coverage. It does not demonstrate renewal of the damper rod's internal seal, internal cartridge bearing or piston sealing parts. Likewise an inspection instruction to replace a base-valve bushing does not by itself supply that subassembly's press/retention method. We must avoid both false extremes: calling an assembly swap an internal overhaul, or claiming stock seal service necessarily requires valving redesign.
