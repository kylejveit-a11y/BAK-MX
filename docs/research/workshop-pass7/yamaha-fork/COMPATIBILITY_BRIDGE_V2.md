## Admission decision

Shared seal/bush identity and AOSS class are substantially supported. The missing bridge is now narrowly D03-23170-00 cartridge-head hardware: whether it uses the illustrated threaded head, the seal's retention/orientation without the obsolete backup arrangement, and the maker method's45Nm head tightening applicability. Existing valve assemblies can remain intact during a compatible rod-seal method; no valve-stack redesign or new shim stack is being demanded.

Do not publish45Nm or add the old backup ring solely from the generic image. A matching current cylinder-head part cross-reference, maker instruction for this newer seal-head variant, or documented physical interface review would resolve the remaining issue. No contact messages were sent and no procedure released.
