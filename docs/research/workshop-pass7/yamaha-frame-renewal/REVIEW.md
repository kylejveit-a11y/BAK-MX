# Frame and chassis bearing renewal composition

Candidate for exact US2026 stock YZ450F. The150step route begins with an assembled bike, removes the engine and supported assemblies, removes steering races to expose the serviceable main frame, renews the listed bearing stacks and restores the bike. It is a source-bound composition, not physical validation or a complete internal engine/suspension rebuild.

Seven approved artifact dependencies retain their hashes. The106step frame route supplies single bike-level removal/refit. Added work uses only approved wheel, steering, swingarm/linkage, shock-eye and throttle-grip access steps. Bench work is grouped after exposed-frame inspection. The bonded frame pads stay installed. No hydraulic line is opened.

| Cutpoint | Renewal / closure rule |
|---|---|
| Shared engine/swingarm pivot | Engine-out withdraws once; arm follows its temporary support removal. Renewed arm stack is positioned before the same pivot locates engine and arm on refit. |
| Rear wheel | Single removal; three bearings, spacer, new clip and approximate1mm seals; disc/sprocket restored on bench. Single axle refit and final torque precede supported dial-gauge runout. |
| Front wheel | Single removal; left bearing then spacer/right, flush seals; disc remains attached. Axle seating/final torque remains after rear wheel and chassis restoration. |
| Steering | Both races removed before bare-frame inspection. New races/lower bearing seated afterward; original preload and fork/brake/control closure retained. |
| Linkage / shock eyes | Bench renewal follows frame inspection, before either upper shock mounting or rear linkage closure. Sealed damper and unchanged spring/preload retained. |
| Throttle | Grip free play recorded while originally assembled. Grip/cable ends released before supported bar removal; throttle body stays attached to its identified cables. Grip/end housing refit follows bar mounting and precedes static routing/return check. |
| Engine operating checks | One initial idle oil-flow check, then warm measured idle and throttle return/steering checks. Engine fully stopped and cooled before cold coolant recheck. No independent control-guide start is duplicated during strip. |
