# US 2025 FC 450 oil service

Twelve original-worded steps cover the complete warm-drain oil/filter/screen service, refill, start/leak check and temperature-dependent level correction. This guide begins with a normally running assembled motorcycle already at operating temperature, then stopped. It does not diagnose a nonrunning engine or remove aftermarket guards.

No numerical filler-plug torque, extra oil specification, socket size or unspecified replacement interval was invented. The source's 1.20L service amount is not treated as a top-up dose. Cold level targets differ from operating-temperature targets; warm checks wait one minute after stopping. Conditional correction repeats leak/level checks and cannot approve an unresolved level.
