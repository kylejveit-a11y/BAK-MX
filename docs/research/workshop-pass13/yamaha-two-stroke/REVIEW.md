# Two-stroke coverage review

Exact standard US 2020-2027 YZ125, YZ250, YZ125X and YZ250X: 32 targets backed by 29 downloaded OEM owner-service manuals. Covers, document hashes and model identities were checked. The public files actually print 2027; revision numbers were not used to infer years. These are recovered sources, not 32 completed motorcycles.

Engine, chassis and fork service chapters are available in the recovered full manuals: these gaps primarily mean source review and procedure authoring are unfinished. They do not mean the evidence is unavailable. Shock internal-service sufficiency has not been audited operation by operation; a manual title or exploded view is not proof of a complete seal/bleed/gas-charge process. Parts catalogs also do not establish executable repair coverage or interchangeability.

The YZ250 bench route starts with a cold drained engine already accessible and a separately accepted lower end. It ends before engine refit, fluid restoration, leak testing and break-in. Thus it does not complete a motorcycle or a whole-engine rebuild. Physical workshop testing has not occurred.

2020 YZ250 differs materially: 5 N·m pushrod/housing fasteners, quarter-turn head loosening, different bore wear limit, explicit installed ring-gap standard and free-play checks. Its OEM cylinder measurement prose says average while the formula says maximum. The approved bounded guide preserves a qualified method/disposition dependency and never silently chooses one. Later years' measurements were not transferred to resolve it. Separate source differences also include cooling capacities/pressures; these are excluded from the bench closure rather than borrowed.

2020 damping restores actual factory marks/ranges where the manual gives them. Later manuals' nominal settings were checked individually. No model-year sharing is granted from equal numbers. Mixed fractions are displayed as mixed numbers rather than apparent subtraction.

Read-only validators bind source PDFs, exact target identities, ordered steps, artifact hashes and independent reviews. They establish data integrity and source-review status, not mechanical test results, complete coverage or launch readiness.
