# One-bike completion audit: standard US 2026 Yamaha YZ450F

This audit freezes scope to the stock 2026 US YZ450F. The existing Yamaha manual is `D03-2819U-10 / LIT-11626-39-20`, SHA256 `b5cbf756d688c8516251e456d4255da4ab607c8e1206e1408f254a79f74a6fbd`. Honda work is paused. No database or frozen source changed.

The bike has useful continuous routes, but it is not yet a complete guided rebuild product. A serviceable-original-part route, a complete-assembly replacement and an internal component rebuild are different outcomes. Do not substitute one label for another.

## Source gaps versus implementation and real-bike work

The clutch and exact shock internals are evidence gaps under active research. Front hub, rear hub, steering and linkage renewal have promising exact OEM source sections and can produce real new procedures without waiting for those blockers. Other rows must first be scoped against the actual source, not assumed absent from the manual merely because no app guide exists.

The full engine phase index still needs a runnable continuous composition after its preflight conflict is resolved. A combined frame/engine/fork/shock job also needs state-aware phase composition: never refit an engine or fork before its selected internal work, never withdraw the shared pivot twice, and never mark skipped work performed. Existing independent routes should remain independently usable.

Actual-world validation remains separate for every route:

- A competent mechanic executes the exact stock bike's path, records parts/tools/access issues, measurements and torque stages, and verifies every diagram-dependent orientation.
- Verify the retained-control/hydraulic access assumption, safe support and engine/swingarm shared-pivot handling on the physical bike.
- Confirm fork bleed/full extension and sealing, wheel alignment/braking, cooling pressure/leaks, engine oil flow, configured YDT temperature/idle and controlled postassembly checks.
- Exercise app interruption/restart, failed-measurement handling, forward skips marked NOT_PERFORMED and post-repair rejoin on Apple and Android hardware.
- Develop rider-tested tuning sessions around a recorded stock baseline, one recorded change and outcome/revert; clicker reset alone is not personalized tuning completion.

No physical wrenching was performed in this audit. Source review, hashes and software checks cannot certify an assembled bike or launch readiness.
