## What the makers do not publish (recorded so a gap is not mistaken for missing work)
- Yamaha YZ65 / YZ85 2020-2027: only an 80-page Owner's Manual exists in Yamaha's library (no Owner's Service Manual). No torque
  table, spring rates, fork oil amount/level or sag.
- Kawasaki KX (all): no sag / ride-height figure; preload is given as the adjusting-nut position only.
- Suzuki RM-Z: nothing free beyond the spec sheet and brochure. Clickers, torques, capacities and the service schedule exist
  only in the factory service manuals Suzuki sells (RM-Z250 2019-2026 #99500-46K00-01E $55; RM-Z450 2018-2026
  #99500-37K00-01E $80, genuinesuzukimanuals.com). Owner's manuals are dealer-only. Suzuki MX-Tuner has no public
  documentation beyond the 2020 press release.
- Honda CRF150R 2021-2026: official spec sheets exist (hondanews.com, powersports.honda.com) but the sites refuse automated
  reading; needs a browser session. Honda Motopub offers owner's manuals for 2018 and 2020 only.
- Triumph 2027: handbooks exist only for TF 250-X / TF 450-X so far (TF 250-E/450-E/250-C/450-C 2027 not yet on the portal).
