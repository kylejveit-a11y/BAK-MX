# What the maker does not publish — 18 November 2026

Ask BAK's gap log showed riders asking for sag targets it could not find. For some bikes the value is not missing: the
manufacturer never prints it. `shared/maker-publishing.ts` records those cases so Ask BAK says so plainly instead of
calling the value unconfirmed and logging a gap that cannot be filled.

## Kawasaki: no sag figure in any US KX owner's manual, 2014–2027

Method: downloaded the text layer (`search.xml`) of every US KX owner's manual BAK cites, 48 documents
(KX65, KX85, KX112, KX250F/KX250, KX250X, KX450F/KX450, KX450X; publication numbers 99987-1770 to 99814-0239),
from Kawasaki's manual viewer host, and searched each for "sag" (the text layer has no spaces, so every match of the
letters was read in context). Result: no manual prints the word. The Rear Shock Absorber Adjustment section sets the
spring by the spring preload adjusting nut position (standard length and adjustable range) and lists the standard and
optional spring rates; those values are already on file as verified facts.

Scope recorded: the bike-years whose own manual was searched. KX85 2027 and KX112 2027 have no manual on file and are left out.
2010–2013 KX250F/KX450F manuals have no text layer on the host and were not checked.

## Not settled here

- Honda and Yamaha static (free) sag: no entry. Their manuals have to be read for it first.
- Suzuki: no manufacturer-published RM-Z manual has been found online.

## Yamaha YZ65 and YZ85: no sag figure in the owner's manual

The owner's manuals for YZ65 2020–2025 and 2027 and YZ85 2020–2021 were downloaded from Yamaha's library and their text
searched for "sag" and "sunken length": no match in any. These are owner's manuals without the service and tuning chapters.
YZ65 2026 and YZ85 2022–2027 were not matched to a manual in this pass and carry no note.

## Honda: no free sag figure in the 2013 CRF450R and 2014–2019 R / RX manuals

Read in full in the browser (see ../honda-sag/REPORT.md): the words "free sag" do not appear in the 2013 CRF450R manual, the
2014–2019 CRF250R and CRF450R manuals, the 2017 CRF450RX, the 2019 R/RX manuals or the 2019 CRF450X. They print the standard
race sag only. The 2010–2013 era manuals print 10–25 mm and that is on file.
