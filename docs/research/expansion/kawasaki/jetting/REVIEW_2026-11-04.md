# Kawasaki KX85 jetting table (2023-2025), 2026-11-04

Kawasaki's viewer serves page images only to a browser session, so Kyle captured the TUNING pages himself (2024 KX85/KX112
manual 99803-0399, printed pages 153-163) and attached the screenshots.

- `kx85c-chart.json`: the KX85C "Altitude / Setting / Temperature" table from printed pages 160-161, transcribed from the
  screenshots: correction factor, air screw opening, slow jet, needle clip and main jet at 0, 1,000, 2,000 and 3,000 m and
  0-40 °C (cells the manual leaves blank are not stored). °F is the conversion printed on the KX112A page (32/50/68/86/104).
- Second read: every one of the 11 cells was matched against the text layer of each year's manual (the viewer's
  search.xml). 2023 (99803-0304), 2024 (99803-0399) and 2025 (99814-0013) print the same 11 cells; loaded for those years.
- Not loaded: KX85 2020-2022 (a different table, not captured), KX85 2026 (new model KX85E, different manual), all KX112
  (printed page 162 was not in the screenshots), KX65 (not captured yet).
