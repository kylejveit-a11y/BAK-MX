# Honda CRF150R 2021–2026 — owner's manuals and competition handbooks, 2026-11-03

Scope: US standard CRF150R (not the CRF150RB Big Wheel, called CRF150R Expert from 2025), model years 2021–2026.
Facts only, brief original wording; every row carries its source URL, PDF page, printed page and the PDF's SHA-256.
No manual text or PDF is stored in the repository. The PDFs were never saved to disk: they were read and hashed in the browser.

## Method

1. `curl` gets HTTP 403 from Honda's CDN, so each PDF was fetched in the browser pane, hashed there (SHA-256 of the bytes) and read
   with pdf.js (text items with glyph positions, grouped into lines; a gap wider than 4 pt separates cells).
2. Owner's manual: the Specifications chapter (dimension, frame, engine, drive train, electrical). The table prints a CRF150R and a
   CRF150RB row for each item that differs. Only the CRF150R row was taken. Items printed once apply to both.
3. Handbook: the engine and frame torque table (2-3, 2-4); the competition maintenance schedule (2-7); engine and frame optional parts
   (3-2, 3-3, CRF150R page only); fork and shock standard adjuster positions and ranges (3-25, 3-35); fork oil capacity and level for the
   CRF150R standard spring (3-30, 3-31); fork height in the clamps (3-32); race sag (3-36, 3-41); spring install length (3-37);
   carburetor clip position, float level and standard pilot-screw opening (3-11, 3-13, 3-20); chain slack (2-33).
4. A path found with two different values in one document was dropped (none came up in the rows loaded). Every torque was checked
   against its printed kgf·m and lbf·ft, spring rates against lbf/in, and fork oil cm³ against US oz.
5. Year to year: the handbook pages used are identical from 2021 to 2026 (same pages, same values; only tool names differ).
   The owner's-manual values are identical from 2022 to 2026 except how the caster angle is printed ("27°2'" in 2022–2023, "27° 2'" from 2024).
6. The schedule page (2025 handbook 2-7) and two specification pages (2025 owner's manual page 48, 2021 owner's manual page 49)
   were rendered and compared with the extracted rows; all matched.
7. Loaded with `private.stage_manual_rows`: 2025 owner's manual and all six handbooks with an empty base; the other owner's manuals
   were diffed against 2025 (page map plus value overrides). The database matched the browser-side SHA-256 for all 12 payloads.
   Rows were then promoted from STAGED to VERIFIED through `public.evidence_approved`, with statement, URL and locator checked
   against the evidence record, and only where the bike-year had no VERIFIED row with the same statement.

## Differences worth knowing

- 2021 owner's manual: bore x stroke 66.0 x 43.7 mm and displacement 149.7 cm³ (later years: 66.000 x 43.777 mm and 149.77 cm³);
  engine oil after disassembly 0.7 US qt (0.7 L) (later years: 0.74 US qt (0.70 L)); API SG or higher (later years: SJ). Each is
  loaded as printed for its year. The 2021 spec table has no transmission-oil capacity, so 2021 has no such rows.
- The handbook's rear spring "Standard spring" range 222–235.5 mm is the CRF150R value. The CRF150RB block on the same page differs and was not used.

## Left out on purpose

- Every CRF150RB / Expert value: dimensions, final reduction 3.733, tire sizes, Canada tires, 56T sprocket, RB fork and shock springs,
  RB fork oil, RB spring install length.
- Pilot screw adjustment range. Page 3-7 gives 1 1/2 to 2 1/4 turns out (slow-jet selection) and page 3-22 gives a minimum-to-maximum range of
  1-3/4 to 2-1/2 turns out. The source prints it two ways, so no row was loaded.
- Optional-muffler carburetor settings (page 3-21: pilot screw 1-3/4 turns), and the temperature/altitude jetting chart.
- Spring identification marks: the text layer prints several of them twice ("1 scribe mark" / "1 scribe marks").
- Fork oil capacity and level for the optional stiffer and softer springs. Only the standard spring was taken.
- Owner's-manual maintenance schedule: its marks are graphics. The handbook schedule, whose marks are text, was used instead.
- The owner's-manual torque line for the fork air pressure release screw duplicates the handbook row.
- Standard (generic fastener) torque table.
- 2027: Honda lists no CRF150R manual.

## Caveats

- The loader stamps `retrieved_at` / `reviewed_at` as 2026-10-10 (hard-coded in `stage_manual_rows`). The real read date is 2026-11-03,
  as recorded in `approval_reference`.
- The extraction ran as in-page JavaScript in the browser. It is not saved as a script in `scripts/manual-textlayer/`, so a re-run
  needs the method above.
- Domain comes from the loader's path rule: `Fork …` / `Shock absorber …` are suspension, `Service schedule …` is maintenance,
  everything else is service (including the specification table).
