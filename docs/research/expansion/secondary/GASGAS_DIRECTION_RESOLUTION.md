# GasGas fork direction conflict resolution

**Resolved for the original standard-model forks on 2025 MC 250F and MC 450F.** The correct setting direction is counterclockwise after establishing the clockwise stop. This is supported directly by each bike's German-language OEM manual; it does not depend on inheritance from another motorcycle.

| Bike | Exact fork | German OEM manual | Compression pages | Rebound page | Identity page |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2025 MC 250F | A460C105Y406000 | 3215221de, 19/12/2024 | 51–52 | 53 | 155 |
| 2025 MC 450F | A460C109Y406000 | 3215223de, 19/12/2024 | 53–54 | 55 | 155 |

Pages are physical PDF pages and match the displayed page numbers in these German editions. Their pagination differs from the English editions. Sections 10.14/10.15 identify the upper-right compression adjuster and lower-right rebound adjuster. The German phrase “gegen den Uhrzeigersinn” explicitly means counterclockwise. The compression and rebound instruction pages were rendered and visually reviewed, including standard-versus-special-edition separation.

The contradictory English sentence must remain in the conflict record. This review found no publisher-issued erratum. Bilingual OEM evidence resolves the operating direction for these exact fork identities, while recording the English publication defect rather than silently deleting it. Do not inherit special-edition click counts or apply this record to an aftermarket cartridge or replaced fork.

Four structured direction records, provenance hashes and approval conditions are in `gasgas-fork-direction-resolution.json`. Database promotion has not been performed by this lane.
