# Non-KTM coverage expansion

Active scope: Yamaha YZ250F/YZ450F, Honda CRF250R/CRF450R, Kawasaki KX250/KX450, Husqvarna FC250/FC450 and GasGas MC250F/MC450F, each 2022–2026 US standard: 50 exact targets. User explicitly lifted the prior KTM-first queue restriction. Paid KTM repair-manual purchase is on hold.

The benchmark is the actual 2025 KTM450SX-F US public-source checkpoint. Other KTM model-years have not reached that benchmark. Parity means comparable verified coverage, not copying its part count or its values:

- Full model-specific catalog enumeration, with a reconciled denominator.
- Full published labels, exact part/callout identity, alternatives and quantity evidence.
- Available owner/service torque and specification tables extracted and reviewed.
- Exact installation mappings with preparation, sequence and applicability preserved.
- Published dimensions, wear limits, maintenance and component data retained with limits.
- Explicit unresolved source conflicts and unavailable requirements.
- Approved records imported, retrieval validated and coverage reported honestly.

An OEM document accessible through a dealer can establish OEM-authored facts after document/scope review. A component manufacturer's instructions can establish its own component settings under exact configuration. A tuner's original measured results can establish those measurements under the reported conditions. A tester's setup preference can be retained as an attributed preference, never silently as the stock/OEM specification. A forum account can be evidence of an observation; independent corroboration and adequate installation context are required before critical technical values are treated as specifications. Multiple sites repeating one source are not independent corroboration.

Public technical downloads, installation sheets, race setup notes, product FAQs and original tests are in scope. Private files, hidden credentials, paywall circumvention and paid purchases are not authorized by this expansion instruction.

Each manufacturer owns its acquisition directory. Raw captures remain separate from the approved import bundle. Existing KTM data is preserved. Missing records and failed access do not imply zero parts or a complete catalog.
