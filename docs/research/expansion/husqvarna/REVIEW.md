Torque tables for the eight 2022–2025 FC 250 / FC 450 US standard targets were visually reviewed against the original OEM PDFs. Source scope was checked on PDF page 3. APPROVED means reviewed source transcription for the stated joint and conditions; it does not establish a mapping to every catalog bolt.

The 2026 documents identify EU standard and US Factory Edition models, not US standard. Those facts remain PENDING. No KTM specification was inherited.

## Explicit conflicts

- 2023–2025 linkage nut thread: manual M16x1.5 conflicts with catalog M14x1.5. These rows are CONFLICTED; no KTM substitution.
- 2025 FC 450 pressure-pump cover screw: manual M6x16 conflicts with M5 component identification; quarantined.
- 2023 and 2024 FC 450 throttle body hose clamp: duplicate manual M4 entries give 2.8 and 5 Nm; both remain CONFLICTED.
- FX-only and Factory Edition-only rows are excluded from standard FC approvals.

## Review method

Rendered every engine and chassis torque-table page for the eight exact US targets. Checked numerical values, units, thread dimensions, multiple tightening stages, Loctite and lubrication conditions against rendered tables. Reconstructed merged multi-stage table cells where text extraction split them. Oil conditions are taken from the torque instruction cell or explicit Engine oil treatment, never inferred from the word oil in a joint name.

Eight exact US standard engine tables visually reviewed: FC250 2022 PDF131; 2023 PDF133; 2024 PDF142; 2025 PDF134. FC450 2022 PDF135; 2023 PDF149; 2024 PDF156; 2025 PDF144.
