## Remaining evidence gaps

- 2026 US standard applicability: official captured standard catalog is EU; OEM manual covers EU standard and US Factory Edition. Neither is promoted to US standard.
- Catalog is the complete service-parts listing when marked complete. Kits, alternate shim sizes and common WP assortment rows do not represent simultaneously installed parts.
- Some OEM article descriptions are abbreviated or truncate dimensions. Preserve original labels; do not invent the missing suffix or dimensions.
- Exact torque-to-installation mapping, internal suspension service torques, material/grade, replacement rules and wear limits remain incomplete. A torque record for a named joint is not a torque assignment to every bolt of its diameter.
- Manual conflicts: linkage thread mismatches, FC450 throttle-body clamp duplicate values, 2025 FC450 oil-pump-cover thread mismatch, malformed 2025 FC450 first-gear value. Conflict rows cannot become runtime specifications.
- Maintenance intervals and full rebuild procedures need further exact-target evidence review. No paid or unauthorized manual access used.
- Aftermarket settings are not substituted for stock specifications; none needed to establish the current OEM records.
