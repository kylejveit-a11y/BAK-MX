# KTM, Husqvarna and GasGas carburetor tuning charts, 2026-11-03

## Why

Ask BAK now works out the day's air (elevation on file for the track plus the race forecast). For a carburetor bike the
most useful answer is the maker's own jetting for that air, and the owner's manuals of every carbureted KTM-group
motocross bike print it: a grid of elevation bands (0-300 m up to 2,301-3,000 m) against air-temperature bands
(-20 °C to 49 °C), with the idle air screw, idling jet, jet needle, needle clip position and main jet in each cell.
None of it was on file (only the CRF150R 2020 Honda chart was).

## What was read

`scripts/manual-textlayer/jetting.py` on the same public English owner's manuals the earlier text-layer passes used
(`docs.tsv` maps each manual to the bikes already on file for it). `charts.jsonl` is the raw read.

- Cells are placed by the x position of the temperature-column headers, so a cell the maker leaves blank (no setting for
  very hot air at 3,000 m, printed "•/•" in the newer layout) stays blank and is not stored.
- A chart is kept only when its elevation bands run from sea level to the top with no band missing, every cell in a band
  has the same set of rows, and °C matches °F and metres match feet where both are printed. Where a layout prints only
  °C or only metres (2025 85 SX), the other unit is the plain conversion.
- Second read: every main jet, idling jet and idle air screw row of every chart was read again with `pdftotext -layout`
  and compared band by band (`second-read.log`). The only differences were cells the manual itself prints with a decimal
  comma ("1,5" turns among "1.5" values); those are stored as 1.5. Needle and clip rows were checked for shape (clip 1-5,
  needle code) and by eye on the 85 SX 2024 and 2026 and the 65 SX 2026 charts.
- Where a manual prints more than one chart, each keeps its printed name: "standard mode" and "lean" (85 SX / TC 85 /
  MC 85 from 2025), "low seat position" and "high seat position" (50 SX / TC 50 / MC 50 from 2024), and one chart per
  model in the shared 125 SX / 150 SX manuals.

## Loaded

78 bike-years, 2,958 rows (`load/`, `load/summary.json`): KTM 50 SX 2024-27, 65 SX 2020-27, 85 SX 17/14 and 19/16
2022-27, 125 SX / 150 SX / 250 SX 2020-22; Husqvarna TC 50 2024-27, TC 65 2020-27, TC 85 2022-27, TC 125 / TC 250
2020-22; GasGas MC 50 2024-27, MC 65 2021-27, MC 85 2021-27, MC 125 2021-22, MC 250 2022.

## Not loaded

- 85 SX and TC 85 2020-2021: the chart is a picture in those manuals (no text layer).
- 50 SX, TC 50 and MC 50 before 2024: no chart in the manual.
- 125 XC 2021-2022: the manual prints two charts under one heading that cannot be told apart.
- Fuel-injected years (TBI and TPI two-strokes, all four-strokes): no chart, nothing to load.
