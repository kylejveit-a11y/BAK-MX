PDF page(s) of that handbook whose text carries it (PDF page = printed page in these handbooks). Handbooks: the US editions on the
Handbooks page of triumphtechnicalinformation.com, fetched without sign-in on 2026-11-06 and hashed (SHA-256 in
`docs/research/expansion/triumph/2027-2026-11-06/REVIEW.md`). A page list was found by searching the page text for the value in the
handbook's own format; "-" means the value is not printed in that handbook. The only "-" cells are in the TF 250-X 2025 jobs, where
each figure belongs to one frame batch and the job names the VIN range it applies to (the other handbook prints the other figure).
