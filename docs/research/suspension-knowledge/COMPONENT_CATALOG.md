# Suspension component catalog: KYB, Showa, WP (2020-2027 in-scope bikes)

Research candidates gathered 2026-10-14 from public web sources; every row carries its source link and a confidence. Nothing here is verified evidence and nothing is loaded into Ask BAK. Part numbers, spring rates and fitment must be confirmed against the manufacturer document before use. Standard and optional spring rates that the OEM manuals print are already verified in the database for most KTM, Husqvarna, GasGas and Honda bike-years (see README.md); prefer those.

Generated from kyb.json, showa.json and wp.json in this folder.

### Gaps

- WebSearch budget ran out mid-task. No searches were done for Race Tech springs and Gold Valves, Enzo, HRC kit parts, Kawasaki/KRT kit parts or Showa BFRC/SFF. Those upgrade categories have no records.
- Some fetches were blocked or refused (permission or server errors): powersports.honda.com product pages (except 2026 CRF450R and the brochures), kawasaki.com spec sheets and parts/oil pages, motorcycle.com 2020 CRF250R/CRF450RX specs, racetech.com, some teknikmotorsport category pages, RevZilla OEM fork diagrams (no part numbers rendered), partseurope FRSP pages (JavaScript only).
- No OEM Honda or Kawasaki fork-seal, bushing or spring part numbers were captured. RevZilla OEM diagrams did not render part data. Look these up on the Honda/Kawasaki OEM parts fiche.
- OEM standard and optional fork/shock spring rates per bike/year are mostly missing. The only ones captured: CRF450R 2021-22 shock 54 N/mm (Showa R38005406), CRF450R 2025 shock 56 to 50 N/mm (MXA), 2025 RWE fork 5.0 / shock 52 (units unclear). Owner's manuals list these, but none for 2020-2027 was retrieved.
- Kawasaki KX250 2025 fork diameter conflicts: Cycle News, Dirt Rider and motorcycle.com say 49 mm. Ultimate Motorcycling and the ADB article text say 48 mm.
- KX250X 2025-2026 is treated as Showa based on aftermarket fitment and a Cycle News note on lighter springs. Confirm on the Kawasaki spec page.
- KX450 2021-2023 and 2025-2026, CRF450R 2022-2023, CRF250R 2023-2026, CRF250RX 2020-2023 and CRF450RX 2020-2022 fitment is inferred from carry-over and aftermarket fitment, not from a manufacturer spec page for each year.
- Kawasaki KX450SR (2024+ limited model) and Honda CRF250RWE (2025+) suspension specs were not researched. Both are likely Showa variants.
- Kawasaki-recommended fork and shock oil for the Showa KX models (SS-47 vs A15-00 vs SS-19) is unconfirmed. The Kawasaki parts pages failed to load.
- No public Showa technical/service manuals for these units were found. Manual URLs for Honda/Kawasaki owner's and service manuals are unverified.
- No evidence that any in-scope 2020-2027 bike uses Showa BFRC or SFF-Air. The 2019 KX250F (out of scope) had SFF-Air, and the 2020+ KX250 switched to KYB.
- Eibach alternate-rate parts (903.052.1, 881.0057.1) have no rate stated on the retailer pages. The rates implied by the part numbers are unconfirmed.
