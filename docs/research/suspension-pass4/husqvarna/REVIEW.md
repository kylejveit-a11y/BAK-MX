# US 2025 FC 450 fork air baseline

The candidate is limited to assembled-bike air-pressure adjustment on F2475Y2. It includes waiting/measurement conditions, unloaded support, pump connection, the exact US row, pressure limits, cap and stand closure, and the initial handling check. Only pressure and handling observations require typed records.

PDF pages 53, 54, 60 and 61 were visually reviewed against the frozen OEM manual. No ambient temperature, cap torque or additional equipment behavior is invented. The rider must have the pump's supplied operating instructions; the guide does not replace that equipment manual.

Compression/rebound baseline is excluded. Pages 55–57 first direct clockwise movement to the stop and then clockwise clicks again, while their own explanatory notes describe counterclockwise as reducing damping. No source correction has been established. Air-pressure instructions are independently unambiguous.

No physical-bike execution, personalized setting validation or internal suspension service is claimed. Existing shock guides remain unchanged.
