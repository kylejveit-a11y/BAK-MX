# Captured assembly comparison — 13 September 2026

The existing confirmed catalog captures were compared without re-downloading or replacing research. `catalog-comparisons.json` binds the exact normalized input hash and lists both original catalog sources for every pair.

582 same-model, same-market, same-variant assembly pairs across different captured years were compared. 220 pairs contain identical captured multisets of orderable part numbers, numeric quantities and alternative identifiers. These are useful research-reuse candidates, not approved compatibility or service-value inheritance.

The FC 2022-to-2023 comparisons contain only four matching FC 250 and three matching FC 450 assemblies, supporting prioritization around the documented generation change. This is an inference about where to review next, not a finding that the remaining assembly changes alter every service specification.

An unchanged part list cannot prove unchanged fastener preparation, service sequence, supersession, machining tolerance, adjustment range or service interval. Nonorderable references, incomplete enumeration, unknown quantity, unconfirmed applicability and unmatched assembly names cannot produce the strongest comparison status. Years absent from a pair are not implied.
