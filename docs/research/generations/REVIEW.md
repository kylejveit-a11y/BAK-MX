# Component year groups and broader off-road roster

Reviewed 13 September 2026. Research only; no runtime roster, database record, torque, clearance or repair procedure was changed.

`component-year-groups.json` records 15 narrowly scoped research groups/boundaries, 15 attributed OEM/component-maker/tuner/regulator sources and a VIN-specific Honda recall exception. Confidence describes the stated boundary or supplier fitment, not complete mechanical equivalence. `offroad-roster-candidates.json` contains 24 family acquisition priorities and a separate unverified future research queue.

## What reduces duplication now

- Honda's retrospective identifies CRF250R 2022–2024 as a useful comparison family, with a 2025 break. US manuals remain the authority for US service details.
- Yamaha footpeg-mount supplier fitment offers concrete component groups: older YZ250F through 2023 / YZ450F through 2022, and newer YZ250F 2024–2025 / YZ450F 2023–2025. These are not whole-bike groups. The product page combines selectable products, so obtain the exact SKU instruction sheet before a fitment answer.
- Pro Circuit names one KX450 piston-kit application spanning 2024–2026. Acquire its installation instructions once, then retain each exact bike identity. This does not establish stock piston clearance or stock rebuild interval.
- Major boundaries documented here: 2023 Husqvarna FC; 2024 GasGas standard MC and Kawasaki KX450; 2025 Honda chassis and Kawasaki KX250; 2026 Yamaha YZ450F. Suspension/linkage changes within those larger generations are explicitly separate.

## Where the four-year shortcut fails

2025 YZ250F revises the linkage, fork internals/settings and rear spring/settings after the 2024 redesign. Husqvarna 2025 revises linkage hardware and frame mounts after the 2023 redesign. GasGas 2025 changes its frame and running gear after the 2024 redesign. Similar paint and chassis ancestry cannot authorize universal clicker settings, rebuild kit fitment, torque or clearance values.

The `models` and `years` arrays summarize a research record, not a Cartesian applicability grant. In particular, the Yamaha footpeg records explicitly exclude 2023 YZ450F from the old group and 2023 YZ250F from the new group. No record permits runtime specification inheritance.

## Concrete current exception

CPSC recall 26-458 (30 April 2026) covers affected VINs of 2025–2026 Honda CRF250R/RX/RWE and CRF450R/RX/RWE. Handlebar clamp rotation requires the official dealer inspection/replacement remedy. Normal tightening is not proof the recall is resolved. See the exact CPSC and VIN-lookup URLs in the JSON. This exception has been communicated to the integration owner; this research does not claim it is active in the app.
