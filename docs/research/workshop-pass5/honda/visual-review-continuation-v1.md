# Exact US 2026 CRF450R — additional visual review

Current official Edge PDF view, 61MKEX50_web, 128 pages. URL and prior capture hash are in REVIEW_PROGRESS.md. Current bytes remain unconfirmed; these notes do not authorize runtime release. No manual images are redistributed. Original concise research notes follow. RWE and RX exceptions are excluded from the selected standard CRF450R route.

## Access and closure

- PDF 10 / 1-4: Remove seat bolts, slide seat forward. Refit hooks to tank slot, rear frame and rear-fender grooves; seat bolts 10 Nm. Standard R shrouds: lower and upper bolts off; refit shroud slot over side-cover tab, lower bolt 10 Nm, upper 5.2 Nm.
- PDF 29 / 2-11: R/RWE tank hanging requires seat and shrouds off. Pinch tank breather using hose clamp, release from steering stem; remove tank bolt and rear band; release clutch hose from guide. Lift slowly and support tank on left frame using intact strap attached to frame and tank stay. Do not load fuel feed hose; protect frame with towel. Refit in reverse with feed hose untwisted/unbent. Frame cushion rubbers must remain fitted in diagram locations; diagram dimension 181 ± 2.5 mm. Restore clutch hose, rear band, bolt and breather; remove temporary pinch clamp. Tank bolt numeric torque is not provided on this page.
- PDF 13 / 1-7: Rear-frame access requires seat, side covers, muffler and hanging tank. Remove air-cleaner lid bolt, disengage tabs and lid.
- PDF 14 / 1-8: Pull ECM suspension tab and lift ECM from airbox without inventing connector removal. Disconnect black IAT 2P, release its wire protector and airbox wire clip. Remove regulator/rectifier and starter/main-relay mounting bolts and assembly. Disconnect crankcase breather at airbox; loosen connecting-tube band at throttle body. Remove upper/lower frame bolts; release tube and lift rear-frame/airbox assembly. Stop there for engine access: subsequent fender/airbox separation is unnecessary for the top-end route.
- PDF 15 / 1-9: Refit rear frame in reverse; upper bolts FIRST 32 Nm, lower 44 Nm. Band hole aligns tube lug; throttle-body-side band screw dimension 6–9 mm as diagram (not band-end gap). Airbox breather clip ears face forward as depicted. Reconnect all previously released wiring/protectors/ECM mounting/breather and lid. Mounting torques not numerically provided for the regulator/relay or lid on these pages.
- PDF 16 / 1-10 standard R/RX: Muffler joint band loosen; remove rear mounting B/washer, front A and muffler/gasket. Refit band tab into muffler cutout, NEW gasket on pipe; loosely mount A, washer/B, secure A before B; joint band 20 Nm. Check exhaust leakage after assembly.
- PDF 17 / 1-11 standard R/RX: With muffler removed, remove head joint nuts, pipe and gasket. Refit NEW port gasket and loosely install pipe nuts, then loosely muffler; pipe nuts 20 Nm, then muffler mounts A before B, then joint band. Do not inherit RWE spring/flange instructions.

## Head / cylinder / piston

- PDF 53 / 2-35: Disconnect ignition-coil primary connectors, remove coil/bolts. Loosen insulator band, support released throttle body without suspending it by hoses/cables. Remove head hanger bolts, plate bolts, plates and collar. Disconnect black ECT 2P; loosen band and remove radiator hose A. Remove head 6 mm bolt, loosen front/rear main head bolts in crisscross order over 2–3 stages. Retain rear washers.
- PDF 54 / 2-36: Lift head enough to remove front bolts/washers, then head; retain chain and loose parts. Remove dowels/head gasket and chain guide. Lift cylinder while supporting piston and chain; remove base gasket/dowels without dropping them into crankcase.
- PDF 56 / 2-38: Clean ring grooves without damage; oil entire ring surfaces. Spacer then side rails; top ring mark upward. Top ring versus upper rail gaps 180 degrees apart; oil-ring gaps 90 degrees apart per drawing. Verify free rotation. Protect crankcase opening with towel; molybdenum oil solution on connecting-rod small-end inner surface.
- PDF 57 / 2-39: Molybdenum oil solution on pin outer surface; engine oil on piston exterior/pin hole. Piston IN mark and large valve recesses face intake. NEW pin clips fully seated with gaps away from piston cutouts. Clean base mating surface. TB1141G (ThreeBond) or equivalent on CASE-side cylinder mating split-line patches shown, 5–10 mm each side per drawing; no bead-diameter inference. Dowels and NEW base gasket.
- PDF 58 / 2-40: Oil cylinder bore, piston exterior and rings; route chain/tensioner through cylinder and compress rings by hand during insertion. Inspect guide slipper for damage/wear; replace if necessary. Guide tabs engage cylinder grooves, lower end engages case groove. Dowels and NEW head gasket; oil front head-bolt threads/seats and fit front bolts/washers to head before lowering onto cylinder.
- PDF 59 / 2-41: Oil rear head-bolt threads/seats and fit washers. Main head bolts 50 Nm in crisscross order over 2–3 stages. Head 6 mm bolt secure. Radiator hose A reconnect; band-end gap 0–0.5 mm as drawing. Reconnect black ECT 2P.
- PDF 60 / 2-42: Hanger plates/collar fitted, hanger bolts FIRST 54 Nm, plate bolts 32 Nm. Throttle-body lug into insulator groove, band hole to locating boss; screw until band ends fully seat on collar. Ignition coil bolted securely; primary connectors restored.
- PDF 61 / 2-43: Exhaust rocker inner and shim slipper surfaces receive molybdenum oil solution; L/R marks outward. Use temporary suitable 8 mm bolt to install shaft with cutout as drawing, remove temporary bolt. Intake rocker inner, cam slipper and shim slipper surfaces receive molybdenum oil solution; L/R marks face right, do not invert arms; shaft cutout as drawing. Then exhaust, rear frame and shim/cam procedure closure. Shaft cutouts also align holder bolt holes on PDF 49.
- PDF 62 / 2-44: Upright level; coolant drain NEW sealing washer and secure bolt. Pro Honda HP coolant or equivalent quality ethylene glycol with corrosion inhibitors, standard 1:1 distilled-water mixture. Fill to filler neck; capacity change 1.08 L, disassembly 1.14 L. Lean approximately 20 degrees each side several times to bleed; replenish and repeat if level falls. Secure cap; shroud upper bolt 5.2 Nm. No invented timed warm-up or numeric drain torque.

## Additional dependency review

- PDF 4 / unnumbered Introduction Symbols: Molybdenum oil solution is engine oil and molybdenum grease mixed 1:1. General grease is lithium multipurpose NLGI 2 or equivalent. The separate molybdenum-disulfide grease symbol specifies more than 3% MoS2, NLGI 2 or equivalent; paste is a distinct symbol. Do not substitute paste for oil solution. The locking-agent default is medium strength unless stated otherwise.
- PDF 11 / 1-5: Side-cover bolt out; slide cover rearward. Refit hook into rear-fender groove and tab into shroud slot; bolt 10 Nm.
- PDF 50 / 2-32: Recommended engine oil Pro Honda GN4 or equivalent motorcycle oil, API SJ or higher excluding Energy Conserving/Resource Conserving, JASO T903 MA, SAE 10W-30. Oil-level procedure starts with three-minute idle, stops for three minutes and checks upright on level ground; lower/near-lower adds toward upper mark. This is an ordinary oil-level procedure, not evidence of an engine-rebuild first-start protocol. Oil drain bolt 18 Nm with engine oil on threads/seating and NEW oiled O-ring; capacities draining 1.00 L, filter change 1.04 L, disassembly 1.35 L. No oil-change operation added to the top-end candidate by inference.
