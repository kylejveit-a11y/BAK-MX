## Required evidence per marketed target

Owner/service manual identity and revision; condition-specific fluids and clearances; fastener-specific torques and procedures; maintenance tasks and severity context; diagnostic codes, electrical/fuel tests and stop conditions; fork/shock identity and setup; gearing and chassis data; ECU/firmware and component applicability. No target is complete merely because one of these exists.

Source URLs, exact scope labels and candidate IDs are retained in coverage-ledger.json. Research facts copied into the staging batch are not counted twice. Rebuild with `node scripts/research-coverage.mjs`.
