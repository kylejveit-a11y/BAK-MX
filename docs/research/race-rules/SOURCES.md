# Race rules sources

Sources behind `shared/race-rules.ts` (version `race-rules-2026.3`). All were public pages, read on **2026-11-07**. No paywalls or bot protection were worked around. The module holds short summaries in BAK's own words, never rule text. The **Label** column is the `source` string the module uses.

Rules change every season. Re-read each source when a new edition comes out, update the items and their `year`, and bump `RACE_RULES_VERSION`.

Downloaded PDFs were saved only to a scratch folder for reading; nothing from them is stored in the repo except the summaries.

## Replaced in version 2026.2 (no longer a `source` label)

- AMA 2023 Competition Rulebook on a third-party mirror (https://www.speedwaybikes.com/AMA/2023-AMA-Competition-Rulebook.pdf), read 2026-11-07. Every amateur item now cites the 2026 AMA Racing Rulebook instead.
- Forum repost of AMA's 2026 change memo (https://pitracer.com/posts/706228), read 2026-11-07. Replaced by the 2026 book. The 2026 book disagrees with it on one point: it gives the youth Supermini class as 12–16, not 12–15 (Loretta's Supermini 1 is 12–15, Supermini 2 is 13–16). It also shows the sound change as a move to the 2-meter max method from 2028, not a one-year postponement.

## Corroboration only (not a `source` label)

- Racer X, "Updates to AMA Supercross Rulebook Posted" (2025-12-23): https://racerxonline.com/2025/12/23/updates-to-ama-supercross-rulebook-posted-including-red-flag-restart-adjustment. Gave the official AMA PDF URL. Confirms the license age moving 16 to 17 to 18, the 109/110 dB/A limits for 2027, and the 2027 chest and back rule.
- Racer X, "New rule for 2026 SMX Next ... chest protectors are mandatory" (2025-12-05): https://racerxonline.com/2025/12/05/new-rule-for-2026-smx-next-sx-amateur-racing-program-chest-protectors-are-mandatory

- Motocross Action, "Ten things about the AMA's new sound tests" (2025): https://motocrossactionmag.com/ten-things-about-the-amas-new-sound-tests. Describes the 2025 method; not used for numbers.
- American Honda statement supporting the 109 dB limit (Total Motorcycle repost): https://www.totalmotorcycle.com/american-honda-supports-109-db-sound-limit-for-2027-season/. Says 109 dB matches the FIM MXGP standard; the 2026 FIM document could not be opened, so the module only states the 2021 FIM figures with a caution.
- FIM news, "Decisions of the MXGP Commission and the Permanent Bureau" (2026-02-20): https://www.fim-moto.com/en/news/news-detail/article/decisions-of-the-mxgp-commission-and-the-permanent-bureau. Confirms 2026 FIM technical regulations exist; no numbers in the article.
- WMX supplemental rules page (https://racewmx.com/supplemental-rules), linked from the Pro Motocross book: on 2026-11-07 it shows only "2027 Supplemental Rules will be posted at a later date".
