## Where makers differ (kept as a spread in the module)

- **Hard pack pressure.** Dunlop keeps the rear at 14 psi on hard and intermediate; Kawasaki's KX manuals say raise pressure on
  pebbly or hard tracks; Mitas lists its lowest range (10–12 psi) for hard terrain. Bridgestone gives one 10–15 psi range.
- **Tire age.** KTM, Husqvarna and GasGas manuals (2026+): change after 5 years at the latest regardless of wear. Dunlop: inspect
  yearly from 5 years, replace at 10 years from manufacture. Both are given.
- **Mousse weight.** Michelin says Bib Mousse is lighter than a reinforced tube; a retailer guide and an independent test put
  mousses at about double a heavy-duty tube. The module gives both. **Needs Kyle's review.**
- **Mousse feel.** Michelin 13.05 psi equivalent (0.8–1.0 bar); Pirelli ≈ 0.7 ± 0.1 bar new; Plews 2–14 psi by compound;
  Nitromousse Platinum 10–12 psi, Plushie 6–8 psi.
- **Terrain words vs compound.** Mitas: Sand = hardest compound, MH = softest. Not a disagreement, but riders assume the opposite.
- **TUbliss core pressure.** Nuetech 100 psi; one tester 110. Module: 100.
