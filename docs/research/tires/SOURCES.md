## Where makers differ (kept as a spread in the module)

- **Hard pack pressure.** Dunlop keeps the rear at 14 psi for hard and intermediate and drops to 11–12 only in soft; Mitas lists its
  lowest range (10–12 psi) for hard terrain and 12–14 for intermediate and soft. Michelin gives one figure (0.9 bar, 13.1 psi) with a
  0.8 bar (11.6 psi) floor. Pirelli's one public event guide uses 14 psi everywhere. The module gives the spread and the reason
  (smooth slick hard pack favours lower; rocky, square-edged hard pack favours higher).
- **Mousse weight.** Michelin says Bib Mousse is lighter than a reinforced tube; a retailer guide and an independent test put
  mousses at about double a heavy-duty tube (rear just under 5 lb). The module says "heavier, about 4.5–5 lb on average" and
  does not repeat Michelin's lighter claim. **Needs Kyle's review.**
- **Mousse feel.** Michelin 0.8–1.0 bar; Pirelli ~0.7 ± 0.1 bar for a new mousse; Nitromousse Platinum 10–12 psi, Plushie 6–8 psi.
- **Mousse in motocross.** The supercross source says motocross riders typically run mousse (world-championship context); the
  retailer guide says most motocross riders run tubes. The module says most amateurs run tubes and some outdoor/world-championship
  riders run mousse.
- **TUbliss core pressure.** Nuetech's guide 100 psi; one tester ran 110. Module: 100.
