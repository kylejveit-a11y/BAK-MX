# Honda and Yamaha evidence checkpoint

Researched 2026-09-12. Ten research candidates, zero production promotions. The JSON alongside this report is deliberately **not** the ingestion schema: it cannot be mistaken for approved runtime evidence.

## Actual evidence obtained

The [Honda 2026 CRF450R/RX/RWE owner's manual](https://cdn.powersports.honda.com/documentum/MWOM/ml.remawmom.amker2626omen.pdf) has readable specification tables on printed pages 73–74 (PDF pages 79–80). Eight narrowly scoped standard CRF450R candidates were recorded: cold intake/exhaust valve clearance, two distinct oil service-fill quantities, idle speed, stock final reduction, spark plug identity and gap. Oil capacities retain their different service conditions; combined-manual variants are not automatically included.

The [Yamaha US 2026 lineup announcement](https://yamahamotorsports.com/Blogs.php?action=blogArticle&blog_id=yamaha-announces-2026-yz-off-road-lineup), dated June 18, 2025, explicitly lists Power Tuner ECU-lock support for 2026 YZ250F and YZ450F. Two separate model-scoped capability candidates were recorded. This is a useful no-start contextual question, not a diagnosis or permission to change ECU settings. Exact app/firmware and operating instructions remain unknown.
