# KTM 2019 owner's manuals and 85 SX 2017 (16 bike-years)

Source and index as docs/research/older/ktm-2013-2018/REVIEW.md (KTM's US manual finder, KTM document host).
- 2019 manuals use the 2020+ layout: read with build_from_index.py (tech_data_2020_2025.py), technical data only; the service-schedule
  rows were left out because the schedule reader reported a mark outside its columns on one page of every 2019 manual.
- 85 SX 17/14 and 19/16 2017: pre-2020 reader. The cover prints "85 SX"; the builders now accept that for the two wheel variants.
- 85 SX 2018 (18_3213629) was excluded after the page check: its torque table sits under headings this reader does not see.
Checks: every row's label and value searched on its PDF page; the only misses were hyphen spacing in jet-needle codes and two-line tire
cells (values correct on the page).
