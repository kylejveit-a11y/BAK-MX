# Suspension foundations intake

Yes: this folder is useful for BAK MX's suspension education, rider-language diagnosis and test logging. Its e-moto emphasis does not prevent reuse of common mechanics. It does not close the app's missing bike-specific rebuild coverage.

The seven current Volume I-VII PDFs total 389 pages: foundations52, springs/sag53, damping57, complete systems52, disciplines58, diagnostics58, professional methodology59. Prefer the current seven-volume set for candidate extraction and preserve earlier files as version history.

## Provenance and technical limits

The manuscripts name relevant engineering books and standards: II p4 lists Cossalter, Foale and Shigley; III p6 lists Dixon, White, Merritt, Timoshenko and SAE J670; VI p7 lists Montgomery, Bendat/Piersol, Dixon, SAE J211 and Cossalter. These are broad reference lists. Sampled material lacks claim-to-edition/page binding and raw case-study records. No external URI annotations were present in the20 PDFs. This audit did not verify the named publications or their support for individual statements; absence of clickable links alone does not invalidate a bibliography.

The final Completion Certificate p1 certifies AI manuscript preparation and labels the set a publication-review candidate. Final Engineering/Editorial Audits p1 and Publication Readiness Report p2 explicitly leave human technical review, citation verification and production review outstanding. These internal certificates are not independent engineering approval, OEM authority or demonstrated workshop competence. Earlier audit counts describe earlier versions.

A concrete equation needs review before reuse: II p12 Eq II-10 adds a tire-force term to transformed suspension force. Without a clearly valid free-body boundary this risks counting series load twice. Keep this formula out of calculation logic pending review; no replacement formula was approved here. The later wheel-rate derivation on p17 is much more explicitly scoped.

Editorial cleanup is also needed: I p51 repeats a template about correcting a bridge chapter with an adjuster. The generic response_index on I p5 is context-dependent, not a universal dimensionless engineering metric. Do not import all prose or equations because the audit says numbering and structure passed. III pp56-57 explicitly describe laboratory training values; VI p55 presents detailed results without an identifiable test record. Treat these as illustrative scenarios unless original measurement provenance is supplied.

## Recommended intake

First curate concise explanations and the volume VI tuning log/question structure. Independently review governing equations and bind factual explanations to exact references. Keep example numbers out of bike prescriptions. Use exact-year OEM/component evidence for torque, oil, pressure, spring limits, adjuster direction, wear limits and disassembly/reassembly steps. The UI can use this reasoning quietly without asking riders to complete an academic worksheet at every step.

No facts or guides were promoted. All41 source hashes were rechecked unchanged after inspection. Temporary extracted text/rendered pages are outside the repo. The inventory and intake JSON preserve status and page locators. Review was selective: all PDFs were inventoried/text-extracted; representative pages and control reports were read, with visual checks of IIp17, IIIp4 and VIp55. Only the unique unpaired DOCX was read; this is not an exhaustive technical proofread or paired-format comparison.
