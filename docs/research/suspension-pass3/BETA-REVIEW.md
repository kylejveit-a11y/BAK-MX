The candidate is restricted to the US 2023 300 RR Race Edition with its original KYB C46. The US release and dated service manual are independently frozen in the review manifest. Standard RR, another year, replacement shock, lowering, and changed valving are excluded. No Yamaha specification transfers.

This is a concise illustrated service companion for a suspension-equipped bench, not a standalone novice manual. Its entry requires an already detached shock. Its exit leaves bike installation outstanding. The original mounting bearings, collars and seals stay intact; their service is outside this operation.

Supplemental evidence is attributed to Race Tech. Its general pressure, oil-level and tuning examples do not override the exact Beta manual. The limited seal-head inference concerns the omitted dust-seal reinstallation action: Race Tech's KYB arrangement matches the reviewed OEM disassembly/assembly geometry. - PDF 35 mentions a Schrader orientation while the stock configuration shown and selected uses the needle-fill cap. The companion retains the original stock-cap orientation and excludes the accessory conversion. No nonexistent Schrader is introduced.

- The source's final pressure uses rounded alternate units. No new conversion tolerance is invented.
- Locking-agent grade is unspecified. Actual product is recorded rather than inventing a numbered grade or cure interval.
- The high-speed setting is restored from the original recorded position; no inferred factory setting is supplied.
- Invalid band size, unknown valving order, damage or incomplete retaining-ring seating prevents proceeding.

Every step requires the source illustration. Measurements, pressures, settings, assembly order and final observations require a work record; routine actions use completion taps.
