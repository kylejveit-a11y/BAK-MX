## Manufacturer manual acquisition routes

- [KTM Manuals & Maintenance](https://www.ktm.com/en-nz/service/manuals.html) provides the manufacturer route to owner manuals and its Print on Demand service.
- [GasGas Owner Manuals](https://www.gasgas.com/en-au/service/manuals.html) is the official manual selector entry point.
- [KTM Print on Demand](https://print.ktm.com/) is linked by the manufacturer for older owner/repair documents, PDF and print. The portal returned no usable text in this research interface. Specific manuals, availability, prices, and commercial reuse licenses are **not confirmed**. Do not claim a purchased manual grants redistribution rights.
- [KTM Motohall archive](https://ktm-motohall.com/en/archive-) offers an additional official archival route and publishes archiv.motohall@ktm.com for missing documents. No email was sent and no purchase was made.

## Real gaps and source-quality checks

1. All 2022–2025 scopes remain absent from this sample. Obtain exact owner/repair manual revisions through the official selectors and archive, not generic model-name search snippets.
2. The US standard 2026 model pages were not established in this pass; search often returned homepages now advertising 2027 bikes. A search title or old URL alone does not establish a historical source.
3. GasGas MY2026 product pages currently contain a warranty block explicitly applicable from MY2027. That block was excluded. This is a concrete example of why entire-page ingestion cannot assign every sentence the URL's year.
4. Numeric suspension-adjuster claims on KTM marketing tables require manual cross-check; no adjuster settings were captured. Never convert a product description into a setup procedure.
5. No source byte snapshot/hash, rights decision, independent reviewer sign-off, or cross-source conflict audit is complete. All 12 records remain research-only.
6. Valve clearances, service torque, fluid refill conditions, inspection intervals, electrical test specifications, diagnostic codes, and installation instructions remain missing here.
