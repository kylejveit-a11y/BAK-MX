# BAK MX Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

BAK MX is operated by Kyle Veit, Tarpon Springs, Florida, USA ("BAK MX", "we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect
when you use the BAK MX app for iPhone and Android and the website at bakmx.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

## What we collect

**Account.** Your email address and password, or your Apple ID sign-in if you choose Sign in with Apple (Apple gives us your email or a private relay address, and your name the first time). Passwords are handled by our sign-in provider, Supabase; we never see them. For Sign in with Apple we keep a token from Apple so we can revoke the sign-in when you delete your account.

**Notifications.** If you turn on reminders, your phone's push notification token, time zone and app version, so reminders reach the right phone at the right time.

**Your bikes and Garage.** Make, model, year, variant and region; nickname and engine hours; parts and work on your build
sheet; suspension, gearing, tire and rider settings you enter (including weight in riding gear and height if you add them);
service history and setup log entries (changes tried, results, tracks, notes).

**Photos, clips and files you add.** Pictures of your bikes, photos and video clips in posts, photos you send to Ask BAK,
photos you Action edit, your profile picture, and any owner's manual you upload for your own bike. They are stored privately and
shown only where you put them: post photos, post clips and your profile picture are visible to other riders; bike photos, chat
photos, your edits until you post them, and your manuals stay private to you.

**Ask BAK conversations.** Your questions, the answers, and a record of each request (the bike details and published
specifications used to answer, the AI model and timing) so answers can be checked and improved. We also log specifications
riders ask for that we don't have yet, without your identity attached to the published data.

**Community and messages.** Your profile (handle, display name, bio, location and riding class you choose to enter), posts,
comments, likes, events you create and RSVPs, direct messages with other riders, riders you block, and reports you file.

**Feedback** you send us.

**Purchases.** Plan, subscription status, product, store and renewal dates; credit packs bought, free question sets started,
and your question and credit balance. App Store and Google Play purchases are processed by Apple or Google and managed through RevenueCat; web purchases
are processed by Stripe. We never receive or store your card number.

We do not use advertising SDKs, do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and do not sell or rent personal
information. The app does not access your contacts or location. It opens your camera or photo library only when you choose
to add a photo.

## How we use it

To run the app (your Garage, conversations, community and membership); to answer your Ask BAK questions for your exact bike;
to keep the service safe (reviewing reported content, preventing abuse, enforcing daily usage limits); to fix problems and
improve answers; and to contact you about your account.

## Ask BAK and the AI provider

Ask BAK builds answers with its own bike data, tuning rules and safety checks, and uses a third-party language model
(currently OpenAI) to write the wording. The app asks your permission before your first question. We share only what an
answer needs (your question, the conversation, any photo you attach, your bike details and setup log, and relevant manual
pages you uploaded), never your email address, password or payment details. When you choose to Action edit a photo, that photo
is sent to OpenAI to make the edit; your original is kept and the edit is saved as a new photo in your account. Ask BAK can
suggest changes in the app (adding a bike, an RSVP, an event, a post draft); nothing happens until you tap Apply, and posts wait
for you to tap Post. That data is not used to train the provider's models.

## Who we share it with

Service providers that run BAK MX for us, only as needed: Supabase (database, file storage and sign-in), Vercel (hosting),
OpenAI (writing Ask BAK answers and making photo edits you ask for), RevenueCat, Apple and Google (App Store and Google Play purchases) and Stripe (web purchases). Other riders see what
you post in the community (profile, posts, comments, events) and the messages you send them. We may disclose information when
the law requires it or to protect riders and the service.

## Your choices and rights

- **Export:** Account → Export my data gives you a copy of your account data.
- **Delete:** Account → Permanently delete account deletes your account and its data, including your photos and manuals.
  Deleting cancels a web (Stripe) subscription; App Store and Google Play subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google, so
  cancel them in your Apple ID or Google Play subscriptions. If you can't sign in, see bakmx.com/delete-account.
- **Edit:** change or remove Garage details, photos, posts and your profile at any time.
- **Block and report** other riders from their profile or post.

Deleted data is removed from our live systems right away and from backups within 30 days. Depending on where you live, you
may have further rights to access, correct or delete your information; email us and we will help.

## Young riders

BAK MX is for riders 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13; if we learn that a child
under 13 has an account, we will delete it. Riders aged 13 to 17 should use BAK MX with a parent's or guardian's permission.
Parents can contact us to review or delete their teen's account.

## Security

Data is encrypted in transit, files are kept in private storage behind short-lived links, and per-rider database rules limit
who can read what.

## Changes and contact

We will post changes here and update the effective date. Questions or requests: support@bakmx.com.
