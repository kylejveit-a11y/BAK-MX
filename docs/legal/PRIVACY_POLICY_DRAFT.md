## What we collect

**Account.** Your email address and password (stored by our authentication provider, Supabase; we never see your password).

**Your bikes and Garage.** Make, model, year, variant and region; nickname and engine hours; parts and work recorded on the
build sheet; suspension, gearing, tire and rider settings you enter (including weight in riding gear and height if you add
them); service history and setup log entries (changes tried, results, tracks, notes).

**Ask BAK conversations.** Your questions, the answers, and a record of each request (the bike details and published
specifications used to answer, the AI model and timing) so answers can be checked and improved. We also log specifications
a rider asked for that we don't have yet, without your identity attached to the published data.

**Community and messages.** Your profile (handle, display name, bio, location and riding class you choose to enter), posts,
comments, likes, events you create and RSVPs, direct messages with other riders, riders you block, and reports you file.

**Feedback** you send us.

**Purchases.** Plan, subscription status, product, store and renewal dates; credit packs bought, free question sets started, and your question and credit balance. App Store purchases are processed by Apple and managed
through RevenueCat; web purchases are processed by Stripe. We do not receive or store your card number.

We do not use advertising SDKs, do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and do not sell personal
information. The app does not access your contacts, photos, location or camera.

## How we use it

To run the app (your Garage, conversations, community and membership); to answer your Ask BAK questions for your exact bike;
to keep the service safe (moderating reported content, preventing abuse, enforcing daily usage limits); to fix problems and
improve answers; and to communicate with you about your account.

## Ask BAK and the AI provider

Ask BAK uses OpenAI to write answers. Before your first question the app asks your permission. To answer, we send OpenAI

## Who we share it with

Service providers that run BAK MX for us, only as needed: Supabase (database and sign-in), Vercel (hosting), OpenAI (writing
Ask BAK answers), RevenueCat and Apple (App Store subscriptions), Stripe (web subscriptions). Other riders see what you post
publicly in the community (profile, posts, comments, events) and the messages you send them. We may disclose information if
required by law.

## Your choices and rights

- **Export:** Account → Export my data downloads a copy of your account data.
- **Delete:** Account → Permanently delete account deletes your account and associated data. Deleting the account cancels a web
  (Stripe) subscription; an App Store subscription is managed by Apple, so cancel it in your Apple ID subscriptions.
- **Edit:** change or remove Garage details, posts and profile at any time.
- **Block and report** other riders from their profile or post.
