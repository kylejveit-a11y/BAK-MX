Build to select in App Store Connect: the newest 1.0.13 build Kyle has checked on his phone (206 or later; it is the first version built with Apple's iOS 27 tools).

## What's New
- Talk to BAK (Pro): tap the blue button beside any Ask BAK box and ask out loud. BAK checks your bike's data, answers by voice, and saves the conversation in your chats
- Say yes to save a change BAK suggests, or no to leave it
- Talk your way through a guided Workshop job: next, go back, repeat, or ask about the step
- Leave the app while BAK is answering and the answer is there when you come back
- Chat clears the starter cards as soon as you send, so the conversation has the room
- Where to Ride on the same finished panels as the rest of the app
- Fixes: Race day, Practice day and Invite a friend now open straight from the menu

## App Review notes (paste into Notes)
Version 1.0.13 adds Talk to BAK, a voice conversation with the same Ask BAK assistant. Sign in with the demo account as before.

Where it is: Talk to BAK is part of the Pro subscription. On a Pro account a blue voice button appears beside the Ask BAK box on Home and in Chat. On the Free demo account the button opens the voice screen, which says Talk to BAK is part of Pro and does not start; Pro is at menu > the account card at the top > Ask BAK plan (StoreKit sandbox works in review).

Microphone: the app asks for the microphone the first time the rider taps the round "Tap to talk" key, and only then. Audio is streamed to our AI service to be understood and answered, and is not stored. The written conversation is saved in the rider's chats like any typed question. This is covered by the same consent screen as typed questions (shown the first time Chat is opened).

Background audio: the app declares the audio background mode so a voice conversation keeps going when the screen locks or the rider puts the phone down while working on the bike. Audio only runs during a conversation the rider started, and ends when they tap End, after 10 minutes of silence, or at 20 minutes.

Limits: 60 minutes of talk time a month on Pro, shown on the screen as a meter.

Nothing else changed in purchases, account deletion (menu > account card > scroll to the bottom > Delete account) or community reporting.
