# 1.0.13 walkthrough on an iOS 27 simulator (2026-11-24)

How: the sample build (sample rider, sample bike, no server) on an iPhone 18 Pro simulator running iOS 27, in a macOS sandbox on Expo, driven by Maestro. This checks screens, navigation and layout. It does not check the live server, sign-in, purchases, the camera or a real voice conversation.

## Seen working
Home (top to bottom, What's New, Edit Home), Garage (Overview, Build sheet, Photos, Coaching, Notes, Setup), Chat (consent, starter cards, typing, sending, the answer, next step, race mode chip), Talk to BAK (opens, idle screen, microphone prompt), Community (feed, new post, comments, a rider's profile), Events (list, post an event form, details, going), the menu and every row in it, Parts (Find, a system's parts, Specs, My list), Workshop (page, guided jobs list), Setup log (list, log form), Service log (list, log form), Where to Ride (list, a place with weather), Know-how, Messages (list, a thread), Race day and Practice day set-up, a started race day on Home, Account (main page, Ask BAK plan, Invite a friend, Preferences, Notifications, Ride safety contacts, How BAK works).

## Fixed
1. Race day and Practice day did nothing when opened from the menu (the sheet tried to open while the menu was still closing). They open now. Invite a friend in the menu opened Account's top instead of the invite page; it opens the invite page.
2. Half a line of page text showed under the floating tab bar on every screen. Content now fades out behind it.
3. Where to Ride was a flat list; it is on raised panels like the menu.
4. Secondary buttons (Setup, Ride here today, Copy my code, No thanks) were flat outlines; they are raised graphite keys.
5. Talk to BAK screen: the talk key is BAK blue, larger, in a rimmed seat.
6. What's New card, the in-app guide and the plan page now cover Talk to BAK; the guide also covers Find a part, the menu and invites.

## Not reached
Supported bikes, Send feedback, Email and password, Your data and legal, Edit profile, Diagrams, where-to-buy tray, a guided job's steps, a Know-how article, the tour, calculators, sign-in and sign-out, purchases, photo and camera pickers, widgets, Siri, notifications, a real voice conversation. The automation could not tap into these reliably in the time the sandbox had.

## Left as is, worth a look
- Ride safety contact form placeholders read "e.g. Kyle" and "e.g. Bella".
- With a form open in Events or Service log, the Publish/Save bar stays pinned while the rest of the page scrolls under it.
- Setup, Photos and Notes in Garage are plain forms and text, plainer than the rest.
