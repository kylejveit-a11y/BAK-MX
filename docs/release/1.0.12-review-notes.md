# BAK MX 1.0.12: App Store submission text

Build to select in App Store Connect: 1.0.12 (194). Its code is commit da0ac202 (git tag ios-1.0.12-build-194). Kyle submits this build whatever main is on.

## What's New
- A new menu: tap the three lines to reach Parts, Workshop, Setup log, Service log, Where to Ride, Ride safety, Race day and Practice day from anywhere
- One Parts page for your bike: Find, Specs, Diagrams and My list
- Find a part: browse by system (suspension, clutch, brakes and more) or search, see factory and aftermarket parts on file for your exact bike, copy the part number, and open the part at a store
- Save parts to your own list
- Invite a friend: share your code and earn Ask BAK questions and free months
- A quick first-time setup that picks your bike and shows you around
- Press and hold any answer, spec or part number to copy it
- A cleaner Account page and a more finished look throughout

## App Review notes (paste into Notes)
Version 1.0.12 is mostly navigation and design. Sign in with the demo account as before.

Menu: the three-line button at the top right replaces the old Account button. Account is the first row of the menu.
Account deletion is now: menu > the account card at the top > scroll to the bottom > "Delete account" > Permanently delete account.
In-app purchases are at: menu > the account card at the top > Ask BAK plan.

Find a part: menu > Parts > Find. Pick "Clutch" or search "brake pads". Each part shows its part number and a
"Where to buy" button that opens that physical part's page on a retailer's public website in the browser. These are
physical motorcycle parts sold by third-party stores. BAK does not sell them, takes no payment, shows no prices, and
earns no commission from these links.

Invite a friend: menu > Invite a friend. A rider shares a code. When a friend creates an account and adds a bike, the
inviter receives 10 extra Ask BAK questions. For every 3 invited friends who become paying subscribers through Apple's
in-app purchase, the inviter earns one free month. Nothing is sold or paid for outside in-app purchase, and no money
changes hands between riders.

Ride safety is unchanged from 1.0.11 and is now also reachable at menu > Ride safety.
