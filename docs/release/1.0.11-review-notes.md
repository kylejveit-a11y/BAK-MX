# BAK MX 1.0.11: App Store submission text

## What's New
- Ride Day mode: big glove-friendly buttons for the track, screen stays on, optional auto-lock with hold-to-unlock
- Ride safety: pick when you'll be back, and your contacts get a link to where you were last seen. Three reminders to you first, then an email to them only if you don't check in
- Right now: live track check-ins (open, closed, muddy, busy and more) from riders at the track
- Crew finder: filter events by level, bike size and riding type, and show you're riding at a track today
- Where to Ride: tracks and riding areas in one list, with what you can ride and how wide, passes, season and rules from the official pages, Near me, Saved places, the Weather Service forecast, events at the place, and Forest Service trail maps for national forest areas
- What I ran here: your past setups, tire pressures and weather at each track
- On-track timer, and your practice time added to engine hours
- Your bike's specs and service list ready offline on ride days
- Tap a rider's name on a comment, an event or a message to see their profile

## App Review notes (paste into Notes)
Version 1.0.11 adds optional Ride safety. The rider adds up to two contacts (name, mobile, email), picks a back-by time
(1 to 8 hours) and taps Start. BAK opens Messages with the link filled in (the rider taps Send) and emails the contacts the link.
While Ride safety is on, the app sends the phone's position every couple of minutes so the contacts can see where the rider was
last seen in a web browser. Background location ("Always") is requested only for this, so it keeps updating with the phone in a
pocket in areas with poor signal; the blue location indicator is shown. The phone shows three local reminders at and after the
back-by time. Only if the rider has not tapped "I'm back" or added time 30 minutes after the back-by time does BAK email the
contacts once, and an all-clear email follows when the rider checks in. Tapping "I'm back" ends it and clears the stored
location. Only the last position is kept, never a trail. Location is not used for anything else; no tracking or advertising.
Location is optional: if the rider declines, the back-by timer and email still work, without a map spot.
To test: sign in with the demo account, Home > Start practice day (any track) > Ride Day mode > Ride safety > add a contact >
1 hour > Start ride safety. Then tap I'm back on the Home banner.

Also in 1.0.11, Where to Ride: Home > More > Where to Ride. Pick "Riding areas", then "Ocala National Forest OHV Trail
System" > "Trail map". The map is Apple Maps with Forest Service (public domain) trail lines drawn on it. "Near me" asks for
location while using the app only to sort the list nearest first; the location stays on the phone. "Open in onX" is a plain
link to onX's public website; there is no partnership or shared data.
