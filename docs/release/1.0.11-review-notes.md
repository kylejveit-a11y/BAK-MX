# BAK MX 1.0.11: App Store submission text

## What's New
- Ride Day mode: big glove-friendly buttons for the track, screen stays on, optional auto-lock with hold-to-unlock
- Safety share link: send someone a link that shows where you were last seen while you ride (free, ends on its own)
- Right now: live track check-ins (open, closed, muddy, busy and more) from riders at the track
- Crew finder: filter events by level, bike size and riding type, and show you're riding at a track today
- What I ran here: your past setups, tire pressures and weather at each track
- On-track timer, and your practice time added to engine hours
- Your bike's specs and service list ready offline on ride days

## App Review notes (paste into Notes)
Version 1.0.11 adds an optional safety share link. Location is used only after the rider taps "Share my location" in Ride Day
mode (Home > start a practice day > Ride Day mode) and picks a duration (2-12 hours). While sharing, the app sends the phone's
position every couple of minutes so a person the rider texts the link to (for example a spouse at the truck) can see where the
rider was last seen in a web browser. Background location ("Always") is requested only for this, so it keeps updating with the
phone in a pocket while riding in areas with poor signal; the blue location indicator is shown. Sharing ends automatically at the
chosen time or when the rider taps Stop sharing, and ending it clears the stored location. Only the last position is kept, never
a trail. Location is not used for anything else, and there is no tracking or advertising use.
To test: sign in with the demo account, Home > Start practice day (any track) > Ride Day mode > Share my location > 2 hours.
