## App Review notes (paste into Notes)
Version 1.0.14 adds ride recording with lap timing, and a way for riders to suggest a place for Where to Ride. Sign in with the demo account as before.

Ride recording. Where it is: Home > Practice day (or Race day) > Start recording. The rider taps Start recording, rides, and taps Stop. The app saves the route and lap times to the rider's account and lists them under their rides. It is free on every plan.

Background location. Ride recording uses location in the background so the ride keeps recording with the phone locked in a pocket or gear bag, which is how a motocross rider carries it. The same background location is used by Ride safety, already in the app, which lets people the rider chooses see where they were last seen. Background location runs only while a recording or Ride safety is on, both of which the rider starts and stops themselves; iOS shows the blue location indicator the whole time. The app asks for location when the rider first taps Start recording (or turns on Ride safety), not at launch. A rider who allows only "While Using" can still record with the app open. Nothing is recorded at any other time, and location is never used for advertising or shared with anyone the rider did not choose.

To test without riding: tap Start recording, walk a short distance or use a simulated location, then tap Stop. The ride appears in the list on the same card.

Stored data. A recorded ride (route and lap times) stays in the rider's account until they delete the ride or their account. The privacy policy at https://bakmx.com/privacy was updated on November 27, 2026 to say this.

Suggest a place. Where it is: menu > Where to Ride > "Know a place that's missing? Suggest it". The rider enters a name, track or riding area, town, state and an optional note, and may add their current location. Suggestions are not shown to other riders. Our team reviews each one and only then lists the place, so no user-generated place appears in the app unreviewed. Text is screened by the same filter as community posts.

Nothing else changed in purchases, sign-in, account deletion (menu > account card > scroll to the bottom > Delete account) or community reporting.
