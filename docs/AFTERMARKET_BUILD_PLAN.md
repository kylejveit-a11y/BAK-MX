## Evidence fields and integration

Record manufacturer/tuner, exact product and revision, part/assembly numbers, source URL/date/hash/locator, evidence author, claimed bike/year/market/variant, required donor hardware and supporting parts, configuration exceptions, installation/service procedure scope and unresolved conflicts. Record performance claims as attributed claims with their test conditions, not established universal gains.

For a rider's modified bike, retain the actual installed kit, spring, tuner/build identifier and map/firmware revision where relevant. Retrieve only matching setup/service evidence. A stock guide remains excluded when its disturbed assembly is modified; the future matching aftermarket guide should replace that dead end with useful instructions when evidence is ready.

Known forum posts, rider reports and company writeups are valuable. Preserve authorship, exact setup and disagreement; distinguish observations from measured specifications. Do not invent compatibility, torque, clearance, charge pressure, spring rate or maps. Public links and authored summaries do not imply permission to distribute complete manuals or proprietary tuner files.

## Sequence and completion criteria

1. Reuse existing candidates and resolve exact identities, starting with common WP conversion and upgraded suspension options.
2. Build exact component/year application groups and their exceptions, with supporting-part requirements.
3. Review complete installation/service and setup routes; preserve source conflicts.
4. Wire installed aftermarket identity into Garage and contextual guidance, then test stock/modified separation and actual use.

No purchase, paid manual, tuner outreach, private setup access or product recommendation is authorized by this research queue alone. Paid KTM manual purchase remains on hold. Research and reversible local implementation are authorized; live purchases and external messages require their own user instruction.
