# BAK MX first-version coverage scope

User clarification, 13 September2026: the first version is intended to cover mainstream racing and trail/off-road bikes from **2020 to the present**, with complete engine, chassis, fork and rear-shock workflows. The old50 non-KTM2022-2026 expansion targets are a historical research batch, not the full release scope.

The current calendar-year baseline is2020-2026. Current released model-years beyond the calendar year must also be checked against OEM availability; existing research notes already observed2027 menus. Menu presence, model-name reuse or a planned row alone does not establish an exact model/year/market/variant or service coverage.

## Roster rules

- Include the existing MX targets plus mainstream two-stroke, cross-country and technical-trail/enduro families. Continue the24 documented family candidates in research/generations/offroad-roster-candidates.json and the user's accepted OEM/aftermarket upgrade track.
- Independently establish which years each model actually existed in the target market. Do not manufacture2020 entries for a model introduced later. Record evidence-backed not-applicable years instead of silently filling them with neighboring models.
- Keep standard US identity as the initial evidence scope. Factory/special editions, other markets, changed forks/shocks and conversions need their own applicability.
- Exclude road variants and the low-volume variants the user placed outside initial priorities. This does not convert an unfinished broader off-road roster into a complete release.

## Meaning of finished

Track these separately for every exact target: identity, parts inventory, engineering specifications, full engine route, chassis strip/reassembly, fork internals, rear-shock internals, setup/diagnostics, source review, application integration and physical/device validation. An operation must include access, service, reassembly and final verification, or clearly state its prepared-component entry condition. A bench module is not a complete bike-level rebuild.

A full-target completion requires all required systems and their repair branches to be covered or explicitly excluded by an accepted product scope. Counts of facts, catalogs, guides or passing tests do not satisfy that requirement. Preserve source conflicts; do not fabricate missing torque, fit, clearance, pressure or compatibility to close a row.
