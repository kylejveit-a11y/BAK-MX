# Affiliate programs: why, who, status (started 2026-11-22)

Kyle said go (2026-11-22). Why now and not after the Google launch: an affiliate product feed is the legitimate source of
product photos and of each store's own stock numbers, which Find a part needs (see PARTS_HUNTER.md).

## What is confirmed
- MotoSport: its own page (motosport.com/about/affiliate) says up to 5% commission, tracking links and banners, and
  "a comprehensive product feed". Needs a website; motorcycle sites preferred. Network and sign-up link not shown on that page.
- Rocky Mountain ATV/MC: an affiliate program exists (a rider announced joining it in 2024). Network, terms and feed NOT confirmed.
- Not confirmed at all yet: RevZilla / Cycle Gear, Partzilla, KTM Twins.
- Correction: the Awin link given to Kyle on 2026-11-22 as Rocky Mountain's was a different company (Kemimoto). Do not reuse it.

## Where BAK's aftermarket store pages point today (2024 KTM 350 SX-F list as a sample)
MotoSport 1,569 · Cycle Gear 1,153 · KTM Twins 259 · brand sites the rest. So MotoSport first, then Cycle Gear/RevZilla.

## Who does what
- Claude: finds each program's real sign-up page and terms, writes every application answer and email, fills forms up to
  the last step, and builds the feed import once a feed exists.
- Kyle: creates each account (password and email check), reads and accepts each agreement, enters tax and payout details,
  and says yes before any form is submitted or any email is sent in his name.
- Rules that stay: links never change which parts are shown or their order; the app says when BAK may earn a commission;
  Apple's and Google's rules on external purchase links for physical goods are checked before affiliate links ship.
