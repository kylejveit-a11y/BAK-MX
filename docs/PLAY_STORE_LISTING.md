## Full description (4000)
BAK MX is a setup and tuning partner for motocross and off-road riders. Tell it what the bike is doing, the way you'd tell your buddy at the track, and BAK answers for your exact bike, year and settings.

Covers most Honda and Yamaha bikes from 2010, KTM from 2011, Husqvarna and Kawasaki from 2014, Suzuki from 2020, GasGas from 2021 and Triumph from 2024. Not listed? Add it yourself and BAK still helps.

ASK BAK
- Describe a problem in plain words: "front pushes in flat corners", "rear kicks on braking bumps", "bogs out of turns".
- Get one change to try, with what you should feel and when to go back.
- Answers use your bike's published stock settings and what's on your bike now, and say so plainly when a number isn't published.
- Is something normal? Ask, and BAK tells you, explains why, and lists the signs that would mean it isn't.
- Race mode gives short, fast answers between motos.

TALK TO BAK (PRO)
- Tap the blue button beside any Ask BAK box and ask out loud, hands on the bike.
- BAK checks your bike's data before it answers, then says it in plain words.
- Say yes to save a change it suggests. The conversation is saved in your chats.
- Talk your way through a guided Workshop job: next, go back, repeat.

YOUR GARAGE
- Your bikes with clickers, sag, springs, tire pressures, gearing and parts.
- A build sheet of what's stock and what's changed, plus bike photos.

FIND A PART
- Browse by system or search: factory and aftermarket parts on file for your exact bike.
- Part numbers you can copy, and where to buy. Save parts to your own list.

SPECS IN RIDER WORDS
- Engine oil amount and type, riding sag, tire pressure, spark plug and coolant up top.
- Your settings next to stock.
- Torques, service intervals and settings grouped by part of the bike, with search.

KEEP IT RUNNING
- Service log with the maker's schedule: what's due and how many hours are left.
- Setup log: what you changed, where, the conditions, and how it rode.
- Step-by-step guided workshop jobs, hands-free.
- Know-how: what every clicker, spring and setting does, softer versus stiffer.

RACE DAY
- Race prep checklist from the week before to the ride home.
- A starting setup for the track and date, with a countdown on your home screen.
- Race-day fuel plan: what to eat and drink, and when, from your height, weight and age.

WHERE TO RIDE
- Tracks and riding areas in one list, nearest first, with what you can ride, passes and rules from the official pages.
- Forest Service trail maps for national forest riding areas.
- Today's forecast, rider check-ins and events at each place. Save the ones you ride.

RIDE DAY AND RIDE SAFETY
- Ride Day mode: one tap to log sessions, setup changes and how the track is riding.
- Ride safety: set a back-by time and share a link to where you were last seen with the people you choose, until you tap I'm back.

RIDERS AND EVENTS
- Post builds, setup questions and ride plans.
- Find ride days, races, practice nights and clinics, RSVP, and message riders.

ON YOUR PHONE
- Home screen widgets with hours, next service and your next event.
- Reminders when service is due or an event is coming up.

PLANS
BAK MX is free to download with a Garage, logs, specs, community, 7 starter sets of 20 Ask BAK questions and 3 Action photo edits to try. Premium adds 100 Ask BAK questions a day and 5 photo edits a month. Pro adds unlimited questions (fair use), Talk to BAK (60 minutes a month), coaching from riding clips and 15 photo edits a month. Question packs add extra questions that never expire. Prices are shown in the app. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled; manage or cancel them in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions.

Always double-check torque values and anything safety-critical before you ride. BAK MX gives guidance; you make the call on your bike.
