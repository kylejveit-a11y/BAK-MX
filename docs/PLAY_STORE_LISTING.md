## Full description (4000)
BAK MX is a setup and tuning partner for motocross and off-road riders. Tell it what the bike is doing, the way you'd tell your buddy at the track, and BAK answers for your exact bike, year and settings.

ASK BAK
- Describe a problem in plain words: "front pushes in flat corners", "rear kicks on braking bumps", "bogs out of turns".
- Get one change to try at a time, with what you should feel and when to go back.
- Answers use your bike's published stock settings and what's on your bike now, and say so plainly when a number isn't published.
- Is something normal? Ask, and BAK tells you, explains why, and lists the signs that would mean it isn't.
- Race mode gives short, fast answers between motos.

YOUR GARAGE
- Your bikes with clickers, sag, springs, tire pressures, gearing and parts.
- A build sheet of what's stock and what's changed, plus bike photos.
- Readiness at a glance, so BAK always has the right context.

SPECS IN RIDER WORDS
- Engine oil amount and type, riding sag, tire pressure, spark plug and coolant up top.
- Your settings next to stock.
- Torques, service intervals and settings grouped by part of the bike, with search.
- Exact-year parts catalog grouped by area.

KEEP IT RUNNING
- Service log with the maker's schedule: what's due and how many hours are left.
- Setup log: what you changed, where, the conditions, and how it rode.
- Step-by-step guided workshop jobs, hands-free.
- Know-how: what every clicker, spring and setting does, softer versus stiffer.

RACE DAY
- Race prep checklist from the week before to the ride home.
- A starting setup for the track and date, with a countdown to the gate.
- Race-day fuel plan: what to eat and drink, and when, from your height, weight and age.

RIDERS AND EVENTS
- Post builds, setup questions and ride plans.
- Find ride days, races, practice nights and clinics, and RSVP.
- Message other riders.

ALSO
- Reminders when service is due or an event is coming up.
- Ask BAK how to use any screen or setting and it walks you through it.

PLANS
BAK MX is free to download with a Garage, logs, specs, community, 7 starter sets of 20 Ask BAK questions and 3 Action photo edits to try. Premium adds 100 Ask BAK questions a day and 5 photo edits a month. Pro adds unlimited questions (fair use), coaching from riding clips and 15 photo edits a month. Question packs add extra questions that never expire. Prices are shown in the app. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled; manage or cancel them in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

Always double-check torque values and anything safety-critical before you ride. BAK MX gives guidance; you make the call on your bike.
